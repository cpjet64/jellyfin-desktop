It does not establish the DLL loaded by a future process.

The job log shows an mpv force-update line with abbreviated `01e2533f`, reports `No patch step for 'mpv'`, and creates `mpv-dev-x86_64-v3-20261018-git-01e2533f31.7z`. Its [reset script](https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake/blob/1f8d2e76255abc48f0456d9be99b4cab9c6566e6/cmake/custom_steps.cmake#L79-L120) conditionally resets to the upstream revision.
